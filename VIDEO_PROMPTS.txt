3D PROPOSAL VIDEO - 10-SECOND PROMPTS FOR CHATGPT (SORA)
=========================================================
Two short explainer films for the NEC proposal, built from 10-second clips:
  FILM A - Phase 1: smartwatch + phone app     (10 clips, ~100 s)
  FILM B - Phase 2: dedicated seizure band     ( 8 clips,  ~80 s)
Every prompt below is COMPLETE ON ITS OWN: paste one prompt = one clip.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO DO IT (read once)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CHARACTER REFERENCES FIRST (keeps faces and devices the same in every
   clip). In ChatGPT, generate these 6 images once and save them:
     R1 "3D Pixar-style character sheet of Ali, a 17-year-old Pakistani
         boy, short black hair, grey t-shirt, slim dark smartwatch on his
         left wrist, neutral pose, plain light background"
     R2 "3D Pixar-style character sheet of Ayesha, a Pakistani mother in
         her 40s, shalwar kameez with dupatta, kind face, plain background"
     R3 "3D Pixar-style character sheet of Imran, a Pakistani father in his
         40s, kurta, short beard, plain background"
     R4 "3D Pixar-style character sheet of Sara, a 10-year-old Pakistani
         girl in a light blue school uniform, plain background"
     R5 "3D product render of a slim dark smartwatch with a round screen
         and teal glow, studio light, navy background"
     R6 "3D product render of a slim navy soft-rubber medical wristband with
         a teal light ring and one round button, studio light"
   When making a clip, attach the matching reference image(s) and write
   "Use the attached image(s) for the character/device look."
2. Settings: 16:9, 10 seconds, highest quality your plan allows.
3. Make 2-3 versions of each clip and keep the best.
4. Join the clips in CapCut / Canva / Clipchamp. Add ALL text, screen
   content, labels and the voice-over THERE. (Sora writes garbled text, so
   every prompt asks for blank screens.)
5. If a prompt is refused because of the word "seizure", it does not use
   that word: they all describe "a sudden medical episode". If still
   refused, soften to "suddenly becomes unwell and stiff".

The escalation chain both films show (also on proposal slide 5):
  ~10 s rhythmic movement -> detected -> vibration + "Are you OK?" (15 s)
  tap "I'm OK" -> cancelled, saved for the doctor
  no tap -> loud alarm nearby + alert with GPS + SMS to all guardians
  a guardian taps "I'm coming" -> everyone is told
  nobody in ~30 s -> automatic calls -> then an emergency contact
  (e.g. Rescue 1122, to decide with NEC) -> everything saved in the diary
  Smartwatch: alerts go via the phone. Band: via its own SIM card.

FILM A - PHASE 1: SMARTWATCH + PHONE APP
=====================================================================

A1 - Ordinary day (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, warm and calm,
  16:9. Ali, a 17-year-old Pakistani boy with short black hair and a grey
  t-shirt, walks home along a sunny Pakistani street with a school bag.
  The camera slowly moves in from a wide shot to a close-up of the slim
  dark smartwatch on his left wrist, which glows with a soft teal light.
  Peaceful, everyday mood. No text, no logos, screens blank.

A2 - What the watch measures (10 s)
  10-second 3D product-explainer animation, 16:9, dark navy background
  with soft teal glow. A slim dark smartwatch floats and slowly rotates,
  then turns semi-transparent like an X-ray hologram, revealing three tiny
  glowing parts inside: a small motion-sensor chip with three short
  arrows pointing in different directions, a tiny gyroscope ring spinning,
  and a green light sensor on the back shining downward with a small
  pulsing heart shape above it. Clean, elegant, medical-tech look. No text.

A3 - The episode begins (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9, evening in
  a cosy Pakistani living room with warm lamp light. Ali, a 17-year-old
  boy with short black hair and a grey t-shirt, sits alone on a sofa. He
  suddenly becomes stiff and his arms and legs start moving in rhythmic
  jerks, a sudden medical episode. Shown respectfully from a medium-wide
  side angle, his face turned away, no distress close-up. The camera holds
  steady. The smartwatch on his wrist glows teal. No text.

A4 - Inside the watch: the AI detects it (10 s)
  10-second abstract 3D visualisation, 16:9, dark navy space, teal and
  orange light. Smooth gentle teal waves flow from left to right, then
  turn into sharp, regular, rhythmic waves. The waves flow into a small
  glowing chip; tiny nodes around it light up one after another like a
  small neural network; a circular progress ring around the chip slowly
  fills; when it is full, one soft orange pulse spreads outward. Clean,
  minimal, no text, no numbers.

A5 - "Are you OK?" - no answer, alarm (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9. Close-up of
  a slim dark smartwatch on a teenage boy's wrist resting on a sofa
  cushion. The watch vibrates strongly with visible ripple rings; its round
  screen shows one large blank glowing button and a circular countdown
  ring that slowly empties. The hand does not move. When the ring is
  empty, the watch flashes orange and visible sound waves ripple outward
  into the warm living room. Tense but calm. Screen blank, no text.

A6 - Alert with location to the family (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, 16:9, night. An orange glowing signal rises
  from a smartphone on a living-room table, flies out of the window and
  travels across a Pakistani city at night as a bright light trail. It
  splits into two trails, each with a small glowing map-pin floating above
  it: one reaches a phone on an office desk, the other a phone on a kitchen
  counter. Both phones light up and vibrate. Blank screens, no text.

A7 - Mother responds: "I'm coming" (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9. A warm
  Pakistani home kitchen in the evening. Ayesha, a mother in her 40s in a
  shalwar kameez with a dupatta, picks up her glowing phone, looks at it
  with concern, taps one large blank button on the screen, then quickly
  hurries out of the kitchen. A soft green light pulse travels out of her
  phone and away, showing others are informed. Blank screen, no text.

A8 - Help arrives (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9, cosy
  Pakistani living room with warm lamp light. Ayesha, a mother in shalwar
  kameez and dupatta, and a younger girl kneel next to Ali, a 17-year-old
  boy lying on the sofa. The movements have stopped. Ayesha gently rolls
  him onto his side into the recovery position, keeps a hand on his
  shoulder and stays calmly with him. The watch on his wrist glows soft
  teal again. Reassuring, gentle mood. No text.

A9 - If nobody answers: escalation (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, 16:9, slightly cooler colours. An empty
  office desk at night with a smartphone ringing and glowing on its own;
  then a second phone ringing alone on a bedside table. An orange light
  trail leaves the phone and travels through the city to an ambulance
  station, where a white ambulance's light starts flashing. Informative,
  not frightening. Blank screens, no text, no logos on the ambulance.

A10 - The doctor's seizure diary (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9, a bright
  clean Pakistani clinic in daytime. A friendly doctor in a white coat
  looks at a laptop showing an abstract timeline and a waveform chart.
  Across the desk sit Ali, a 17-year-old boy in a grey t-shirt, and his
  mother Ayesha in shalwar kameez and dupatta. The doctor turns the laptop
  toward them and nods. Hopeful mood. Screen abstract, no readable text.

FILM B - PHASE 2: DEDICATED SEIZURE BAND (NO PHONE NEEDED)

B1 - The band (10 s)
  10-second 3D product animation, 16:9. A slim, rugged, soft-rubber

  The camera slowly circles it. No text, no logos.

B2 - Exploded view: what is inside (10 s)
  10-second 3D product-explainer animation, 16:9, dark navy background,

  its parts floating in layers: a small main processor chip in the centre,
  a tiny motion-sensor chip, a green optical sensor on the underside, a
  mini SIM card with a thin antenna, a small GPS antenna, a flat curved
  battery, a tiny vibration motor, a tiny round speaker and a round
  button. The parts hover for a moment, then smoothly reassemble into the

B3 - Worn by a child at school (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9, bright
  sunny Pakistani school courtyard. Sara, a 10-year-old girl in a light
  blue school uniform, plays with classmates, wearing a navy wristband with
  a teal light ring. The camera moves to a close-up of the band on her
  wrist; a tiny light on its underside glows softly against her skin.
  Cheerful, normal school day. No text.

B4 - Episode and detection (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9. In a sunny
  school courtyard, Sara, a 10-year-old girl in a light blue uniform,
  sits under a tree and suddenly becomes stiff with rhythmic jerking of
  her arms and legs, a sudden medical episode, shown respectfully from a
  distance. A teacher in the background turns her head. The camera moves
  to the navy wristband: inside it, glowing teal waves turn into sharp
  rhythmic waves and a tiny chip lights up orange. No text.

B5 - No answer: alarm (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9, school
  courtyard. Close-up of a navy wristband on a young girl's wrist: it
  vibrates and its teal light ring slowly counts down; nobody touches it.
  Then the ring turns orange, flashes, and visible sound waves ripple
  outward. A teacher in a shalwar kameez and two students run toward the

B6 - Direct alert over the mobile network (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, 16:9. An orange glowing signal rises
  directly from a navy wristband on a child's wrist, straight up to a
  mobile network tower on a rooftop (no phone involved), then splits into
  two light trails across the city, each with a small floating map-pin:
  one reaches a phone in a home, the other a phone in a small shop. Both
  phones light up and ring. Blank screens, no text.

B7 - Care and the record (10 s)
  10-second 3D animated clip, stylized Pixar-like style, 16:9. Under a
  tree in a school courtyard, a teacher has gently placed Sara, a
  10-year-old girl in a blue uniform, on her side in the recovery
  position and stays with her. Her mother, in shalwar kameez and dupatta,
  arrives through the school gate and hurries over. The wristband glows
  calm teal. Soft transition to a doctor's laptop with an abstract
  timeline. Reassuring mood. No text.

B8 - Closing: two devices, one mission (10 s)
  10-second 3D product animation, 16:9, dark navy background with soft
  teal light. A slim dark smartwatch on the left and a navy medical
  wristband on the right float side by side and slowly rotate, gently
  glowing. The camera slowly pulls back, leaving empty space above them
  for a title. Elegant, hopeful. No text, no logos.
